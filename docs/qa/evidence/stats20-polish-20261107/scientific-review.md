- CDC requires WTDR2D for analyses involving both recalls. A unique-SEQN-aligned supplement supplies it without changing the original 29-column extract. Two-day means and both calibration regressions now use it; unweighted variants honor their weight choice.
- Calibration uncertainty includes estimation of both regressions through joint design delta-method linearization. Independent PSU weight perturbations refitting both stages reproduce the variance. Weak calibration and unverifiable replicate/covariate error assumptions limit these approximations.
- Two-recall correlation is distinguished from reliability. Classical attenuation needs independent mean-zero exposure error and appropriate covariate assumptions. Noisy energy and correlated recall errors complicate that interpretation, including in the World generator. Differences between simulated analyses are not presented as an additive causal decomposition.
- The World design uses PPS town draws with replacement and exact stratified age-group inclusion probabilities, with Hansen–Hurwitz draw weights, replacing an approximate unequal-probability without-replacement rule. Across 200 draws, recall-day, joint calibration and true-intake slopes average 0.2264, 0.7399 and 0.9555, with structural-effect coverage 0%, 69.5% and 53.5%. Diagnosis, medication selection and the fitted projection differ from the structural causal parameter.
- The plotted sodium-by-age slopes use the centered main coefficient from the actual interaction fit, not the primary model's coefficient. Spline testing uses design F(2,15): p = 0.02194, replacing the asymptotic chi-square p = 0.00687. The displayed design effect and its SRS SE now share the same weighted-population reference. Decile means use design domain scores retaining other PSUs as zero contributions. Specification intervals use each fit's design t critical value.
- Recall-day association, usual-intake association and randomized intervention effects have different estimands. Non-significance is not equality to zero; diagnostics do not prove a model correct; regression weighting has conditional-mean/sampling assumptions. The causal diagram separates earlier and current pressure to remove directed cycles. Randomization balances baseline confounders in expectation; the meta-analysis is not described as exclusively controlled feeding.
- The plan is an illustrative teaching sequence, not claimed prospective registration. Operational eligibility retains unknown pregnancy and skipped medication codes as no recorded positive/use, not confirmed negatives. Complete-case and nonresponse assumptions limit population generalization. Problems specify conditional test independence and do not attribute the absence of significant results to correlation or transfer a power calculation to a different effect size.
