# QA amendment: post hoc corrected rerun

The original `PROTOCOL.md` is preserved verbatim. This amendment is written after the original displayed results have been seen. It is **not a new preregistration**, and revised evidence must not be called independently preregistered confirmation. Repository history contains the original protocol and source together in cba24c25; that alone does not establish an independently timestamped pre-analysis registration or the timing of the historical run.

Before the corrected rerun, the QA design is recorded here:

- Keep the original 3 shifts × 3 noise settings × 5 methods and 1,000 replicates per cell, all original RNG seeds and targets. Each method's cell has its own simulations; method differences are not paired.
- Check logistic convergence rather than silently returning the last iterate. Use closed-form Gaussian-tilt TV and a piecewise truncated-normal calculation for the absolute-value classifier, checked independently against numerical quadrature. A finite grid integral is an approximation, not “exact for any weight function.”
- Sort calibration scores once and batch the test-weight quantiles, retaining the test atom at infinity. Report median **finite** widths and the infinite fraction separately, plus empirical Monte Carlo error across independent replicate means. An infinite interval occurs when the normalized test weight exceeds alpha, not only when it exceeds the whole calibration mass.
- The NHANES demonstration becomes a fixed empirical-distribution resampling experiment, not an inference claim about national survey coverage or a finite population sampled without replacement. Define source mass proportional to the designed age-selection probability and target mass proportional to its complement. Draw model-training, calibration, auxiliary source/target classifier samples, and target test samples independently **with replacement**. Weight fitting must be independent of calibration and test samples. Record all sample sizes and explain repeated rows.
- Compare original protocol predictions with revised data explicitly, including the uncertainty of coverage-minus-bound. The proof is the theorem's justification; finitely many simulated cells cannot prove it. Infinite intervals contribute coverage but are not, by themselves, a complete causal explanation of all observed excess coverage.
- Save empirical MC errors in all four Coverage Board rows. Generate Part 65's Board before the full-course mosaic, record every input Board hash, and derive totals and quoted historical rows from current inputs. Mosaic verdicts are descriptive three-SE screening labels across heterogeneous simulations, not simultaneous validation of the whole course.
- State score/model independence, positivity, integrability and absolute-continuity conditions in the proof. Estimated-weight conditional bounds require independent weight training from both calibration and test observations; after averaging over weight training the penalty is expected TV. The Gaussian root-n statement requires a regular fixed-dimensional correctly specified classifier with overlap and adequate sample growth.
- Audit attribution: the bound is a teaching derivation/reformulation of established weighted-conformal robustness ideas. No novel theorem or exhaustive literature-gap claim is established by this lesson.
