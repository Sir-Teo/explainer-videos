## The model theorem

Let `(X,M)` be a finite-state Markov-additive process, with integrable absolute first-move rewards. Let `Q` contain no-move transitions, `T` move transitions and `R` one-step expected price increment, so `Q+T` is stochastic. Assume `rho(Q)<1` and that `B=(I-Q)^(-1)T` is stochastic, irreducible and aperiodic on the actual support. Then `g1=(I-Q)^(-1)R` is the expected first-move reward. Centering `pi' g1=0` is essential. Mirror symmetry is sufficient for centering, but is not necessary.

With `Pi=1 pi'` and `A=B-Pi`, the centered adjustment is

`g = sum(k>=0) B^k g1 = (I-B+Pi)^(-1)g1`, with `pi' g=0`.

For every `rho` satisfying `rho(A)<rho<1`, a finite `C_rho` bounds `||B^k g1|| <= C_rho rho^k ||g1||`. A bound using exactly the second eigenvalue modulus can fail with Jordan blocks; diagonalizability gives the stronger bound when applicable. The Poisson equation `g=g1+Bg` makes `M+g(X)` a martingale at move times. Solutions differ by an additive constant, fixed by stationary-mean-zero normalization or by antisymmetry under valid mirror symmetry. Physical-clock conclusions require the corresponding timing and integrability conditions.

The stored estimator sums 401 terms, comparing its result with the first 201 terms. All 20 nominal states have positive counts in this restored fit, but some two-tick states are sparse. Its spectrum, near-zero stationary reward and small series/inverse residual are algebraic diagnostics. They do not certify the observed market's Markov hypothesis. A count-weighted next-sign gap can cancel across states; neither a small gap nor small bucket drift establishes full conditional martingale behavior. The fitted simulator also has a coarse projection, so its theorem assumptions are approximate rather than automatic.

## Worked problems: six basic

1. **Fluid queue time.** Five BTC are ahead. Trades consume 0.2 BTC/s and cancellations remove 0.1 BTC/s from ahead. Your one-BTC order is never cancelled. Assume fixed fluid rates, no new priority ahead and enough available trading volume. Ahead size is `max(5-0.3t,0)`, reaching zero at `5/0.3=16.6667 s`. Your own quantity then fills only through trades, taking `1/0.2=5 s`, so total full-fill time is **21.6667 s**. Cancellations of other orders do not execute yours. This is deterministic arithmetic, not the expected hitting time of a stochastic queue.

2. **Queue-depletion probability.** Independent driftless Brownian queues have equal variance rates and start at bid 9, ask 3. In polar angle `theta=atan(3/9)`, the bounded harmonic exit probability is `u=1-2 theta/pi`, with boundary values one at ask depletion and zero at bid depletion. Thus `u=0.7951672`. The imbalance heuristic is `9/(9+3)=0.75`; they are different models. Multiplying both queues by the same positive constant leaves both probabilities unchanged. Brownian scaling multiplies the depletion-time distribution by the square of that constant; no finite expected depletion time is asserted. Unequal variances, correlation or drift change the simple angle formula.

3. **Stationary Hawkes arithmetic.** In a positive linear Hawkes cluster model, immigration is 2/s and branching is `n<1`. Expected generation sizes are `1,n,n^2,...`, so expected total family size is `1/(1-n)`. At `n=0.68`, this is 3.125 and mean rate is **6.25/s**. At `n=0.95`, family size is 20 and rate **40/s**. Latent offspring shares are 68% and 95% in this model, not identified causal trader-reaction fractions. At one the expected family size diverges and no finite positive stationary mean follows; above one the stationary-mean formula is invalid.

4. **Variance of persistent signs.** Suppose stationary centered unit-variance signs have covariance `C(l) ~ c l^(-gamma)`, with `c>0` and `0<gamma<1`. Expanding the square gives `Var(S_n)=n+2 sum(l=1..n-1)(n-l)C(l)`. Riemann-sum asymptotics give `sum(n-l)l^(-gamma) ~ n^(2-gamma)/[(1-gamma)(2-gamma)]`. Hence `Var(S_n) ~ 2c n^(2-gamma)/[(1-gamma)(2-gamma)]`, dominating `n`. At `gamma=0.85`, the exponent is **1.15**. A finite fitted ACF slope does not prove these asymptotic assumptions. Translating sign-sum growth into price behavior needs an impact/liquidity model; it does not follow from this identity alone.

5. **Stipulated OFI impact.** Under the chosen constant-depth relation, `Delta m = delta E/(2 qbar)`, with tick `$0.10`, average queue 8 BTC and best-quote OFI `+12 BTC`. The slope is `$0.00625/BTC`, yielding **+$0.075 = +0.75 tick**. With depth 16 BTC, the same OFI gives `$0.0375 = 0.375 tick`. Units cancel BTC. This is a consequence of the stipulated model, not a fitted causal response to a counterfactual order.

6. **Interview book.** Bid is `$100.00` for 500 shares, ask `$100.01` for 100. The mid is **$100.005**. Opposite-size weighting gives `(500*100.01+100*100)/600 = $100.0083333`, toward the thin offer. Under the Brownian assumptions in problem 2, `p_up=1-(2/pi)atan(1/5)=0.8743341`; if first depletion changes the midpoint by exactly plus/minus half a cent, expected next midpoint is `100.005+0.005(2p_up-1) = $100.0087433`. A many-move adjustment needs its transition model and normalization. Applying the lecture's simulator bucket as a dimensionless illustration gives about $100.008, but it is not an independently calibrated stock valuation. None of these quantities is a guaranteed executable price.

## Worked problems: four advanced

7. **Conditional accuracy versus costs.** Eighty correct signs among 100 nonzero future targets means **80% conditional accuracy**. Flat targets are excluded, so all-observation accuracy cannot be recovered without their count, predictions and scoring rule. Suppose selected gross mid-to-mid gain is 1.5 ticks, with an immediate market entry and exit each paying half of a one-tick spread plus three ticks of fees. Net under these assumptions is `1.5-0.5-0.5-3-3 = -5.5 ticks`, and break-even gross gain is 7 ticks. This ignores impact and latency and treats selections as comparable round trips. Overlapping forecasts need position accounting. Report the label, selection frequency, base rates, all-target errors, split, costs and dependence-aware uncertainty rather than equating accuracy with profit.

8. **Projection of a Markov queue.** Full states `(1,1)` and `(10,10)` share imbalance `I=1/2`. Remove one bid unit with probability 0.2, otherwise do nothing; bid depletion moves the mid down half a tick. The first full state has next-step down-move probability **0.2** and expected mid change **-0.1 tick**; the second has probability and expected move zero. Thus two full states in the same projected state have different transition/reward laws. Strong lumpability, which would require equal total transition probabilities into every projected class, fails. The full chain can be Markov while imbalance alone is not a sufficient Markov-additive state. This counterexample does not say every projection fails or every Markov model lacks clustering.

9. **Replay accounting.** Suppose back fill probability is 0.82 and conditional signed one-second markout is -14.7 ticks. Assign unfilled orders zero; contribution is `0.82*(-14.7) = -12.054 ticks/submission`. It already includes entry half-spread, so adding another half-tick is incorrect. If a separate chosen example has fill probability 0.8, markout -2 and rebate +0.1 tick/fill, contribution is **-1.52 ticks/submission**. A front/back difference compares these specified gross functionals on different fill sets. Inventory liquidation, missed opportunities, fees, adverse selection beyond the mark horizon and market reaction are still absent. It is not a complete net-profit or causal queue-priority estimate.

10. **Cancellation need not improve markout.** Consider equally frequent states A and B. All orders would fill, with markouts -1 tick in A and +3 in B. Without cancellation, mean markout and per-submission contribution are `(-1+3)/2 = +1 tick`. A misspecified signal leans against B only; cancelling B reduces fill rate from one to one half, conditional markout from +1 to -1, and contribution to `0.5*(-1)=-0.5 tick`. Thus no improvement is guaranteed. Evaluate a frozen, latency-feasible cancellation policy on separate data, with matched order opportunities, executable cancellation and queue rules, fill-selection changes, fees, inventory and liquidation. Keep all-submission value alongside conditional filled markouts and account for dependence; do not choose the rule on the evaluation sample.

## Worked problems: two qualifying

11. **Centered microprice theorem.** Use the setting and integrability assumptions stated above. First-step conditioning gives `g1=R+Qg1`; since `rho(Q)<1`, `g1=(I-Q)^(-1)R`. Summing the no-move waiting paths gives post-move law `B=(I-Q)^(-1)T`. Since `(Q+T)1=1`, `B1=(I-Q)^(-1)(I-Q)1=1`. Strong Markov conditioning at successive moves yields `E_x[M_tau_n]-M_0 = sum(k=0..n-1)(B^k g1)(x)`.

   Let mirror permutation `J` commute with `B` and reverse `g1`: `Jg1=-g1`. Uniqueness of the stationary distribution implies `pi'J=pi'`, so `pi'g1=pi'Jg1=-pi'g1=0`. The same centering may hold without any mirror. Set `Pi=1 pi'`; stochasticity and stationarity give `B Pi=Pi B=Pi`, and for `k>=1`, `(B-Pi)^k=B^k-Pi`. The eigenvalues of `A=B-Pi` are zero and B's nonstationary eigenvalues, all with modulus below one. In finite dimension, Jordan form gives for any `rho(A)<rho<1` a finite constant bounding `||A^k|| <= C_rho rho^k`; polynomial Jordan factors are absorbed by the strict spectral margin. An exact `rho(A)^k` bound is not valid in general. Since `Pi g1=0`, `B^k g1=A^k g1`, including `k=0` after applying to `g1`. Absolute convergence follows.

   The Neumann sum equals `g=(I-A)^(-1)g1=(I-B+Pi)^(-1)g1`, with `pi'g=0`. It solves `(I-B)g=g1` and therefore `g=g1+Bg`. Conditional expectation of `M_tau_(n+1)+g(X_tau_(n+1))` given the move-time past is `M_tau_n+g1(X_tau_n)+(Bg)(X_tau_n)=M_tau_n+g(X_tau_n)`. Integrability of the increments and bounded finite-state `g` makes this a martingale. If `f` is another integrable state correction, `(I-B)(f-g)=0`. A finite irreducible chain's maximum principle makes its harmonic vectors constant, so `f=g+c1`. Stationary mean zero fixes `c=0`; mirror antisymmetry also fixes it because `J1=1`. If `d=pi'g1` is nonzero, decompose `g1=d1+h`; the expected n-move sum is `nd1` plus a bounded centered term, so the uncentered limit diverges. No assertion of fundamental value, executable arbitrage or a physical-time martingale follows without additional structure.

12. **Two-state model and singular limits.** Let `B=[[p,1-p],[1-p,p]]`, `0<p<1`, with stationary law `(1/2,1/2)`. At a move from H the increment is +1 tick with probability q and -1 otherwise; L has the mirror law. Thus `g1=(2q-1)(1,-1)`. The antisymmetric vector is an eigenvector with eigenvalue `lambda=2p-1`, whose modulus is below one. Its series gives `g_H=(2q-1)/(1-lambda)=(2q-1)/[2(1-p)]` and `g_L=-g_H`. At `p=q=3/4`, the lean is **one full tick**. For fixed `q>1/2`, `g_H` tends to positive infinity as `p` tends to one; at one, B is reducible and the stationary-centered inverse is no longer available. If `q=1/2`, `g1=0` and the normalized solution is zero for each `0<p<1`; the divergent-limit claim therefore needs nonzero reward. At `p=0`, the chain is periodic; for nonzero reward the ordinary geometric partial sums oscillate, although the algebraic Poisson equation has a centered solution. A negative eigenvalue can cause alternating terms and must not be interpreted as a positive persistence multiplier. Bitcoin's fitted second modulus near 0.668 suggests a mode-specific geometric multiplier only when the reward has a component in the relevant positive eigenmode; it is not proof that the entire lean scales by three or that the market obeys this two-state model.

### 基础题六道

1. **流体队列时间。** 假设前方5 BTC仅按固定速度减少，主动成交0.2 BTC/秒，前方撤单0.1 BTC/秒，无新优先订单加入。前方数量为 `max(5-0.3t,0)`，到队首需 `5/0.3=16.6667秒`。自己的1 BTC只能由成交消耗，再需 `1/0.2=5秒`，全部成交共 **21.6667秒**。别人的撤单不会执行自己的订单。这里是确定性流体计算，不是随机队列首达时间的期望。

2. **耗尽概率。** 两侧为独立、无漂移、相同方差速率的布朗队列，买9、卖3。象限中调和退出概率在卖队列耗尽边界为1、买队列耗尽边界为0，故 `u=1-(2/pi)atan(3/9)=0.7951672`。不平衡启发式为 `9/12=0.75`，两者模型不同。两队列同倍放大不变概率；布朗缩放使耗尽时间分布按倍数平方缩放，这不保证耗尽时间有有限期望。不同方差、相关或漂移会改变简单角度公式。

3. **平稳Hawkes。** 正线性簇模型的移民率为2/秒，分支比 `n<1`。各代期望数为 `1,n,n²,...`，总家族含移民为 `1/(1-n)`。n=0.68时家族3.125、均速率 **6.25/秒**；n=0.95时家族20、速率 **40/秒**。68%与95%是该模型潜在后代占比，不是被识别的交易者因果反应比例。n=1时期望家族发散，不能推出有限正平稳均值；n>1时平稳均值公式无效。

4. **符号和方差。** 设平稳、中心化、单位方差符号协方差 `C(l)~c l^(-gamma)`，c>0且0<gamma<1。展开平方得 `Var(S_n)=n+2 sum_(l<n)(n-l)C(l)`。黎曼和给出 `sum(n-l)l^(-gamma)~n^(2-gamma)/[(1-gamma)(2-gamma)]`，故主项为 `2c n^(2-gamma)/[(1-gamma)(2-gamma)]`，快于n增长。gamma=0.85时指数 **1.15**。有限窗口拟合斜率不能证明这些渐近假设；由符号和推断价格还需要冲击及流动性模型。

5. **规定OFI关系。** 在固定深度关系 `Delta m=delta E/(2 qbar)` 下，价格档0.10美元、平均队列8 BTC、OFI为+12 BTC。系数0.00625美元/BTC，所以 **+0.075美元=+0.75档**。深度变16 BTC时为0.0375美元=0.375档。BTC单位抵消。这是规定模型的算术推论，不是反事实订单的实证因果冲击。

6. **面试盘口。** 买价100美元500股，卖价100.01美元100股，中点 **100.005美元**。相反侧数量加权为 `(500×100.01+100×100)/600=100.0083333美元`，偏向薄卖队列。在第2题布朗假设下，上移概率 `1-(2/pi)atan(1/5)=0.8743341`；若首次耗尽使中点严格上或下0.005美元，则预期下一中点 `100.005+0.005(2p-1)=100.0087433美元`。多次变动微价格另需转移模型与归一化。把本讲模拟器分箱调整无量纲搬到股票仅得约100.008美元的示例，不是独立校准估值；这些量都不保证可成交。

### 进阶题四道

7. **准确率和成本。** 100个非零未来目标中80个方向正确，条件准确率 **80%**。平盘样本被排除；没有其数量、预测和计分方式，无法恢复全样本准确率。设选中交易中点到中点毛收益1.5档，立即主动入场及退出各付半档价差和3档费用，净额 `1.5-0.5-0.5-3-3=-5.5档`，毛收益盈亏平衡需7档。仍忽略冲击、延迟；重叠预测另需持仓核算。报告标签、选择频率、基准、全目标误差、划分、成本和考虑相关性的区间，不把准确率当作盈利。

8. **马尔可夫投影反例。** 完整状态(1,1)和(10,10)都有不平衡1/2。下一步以0.2概率移除一个买方单位，否则不变；买队列耗尽使中点降半档。第一状态下移概率 **0.2**，期望变动 **-0.1档**；第二状态两者为零。同一投影状态内，转移和奖励规律不同，因而不满足强可合并性要求的“进入每个投影类的总概率相同”。完整链可以是马尔可夫的，但不平衡不是充分的马尔可夫可加状态。此反例不意味着所有投影都失败，也不意味着马尔可夫过程不能聚集或有厚尾。

9. **回放核算。** 成交概率0.82、条件一秒有符号标记收益-14.7档，未成交赋零，提交订单贡献 `0.82×(-14.7)=-12.054档/笔提交`。标记收益已经包含入场半价差，不能再加半档。另一规定例子成交0.8、标记-2、每次成交返佣+0.1，贡献 **-1.52档/笔提交**。前后位置之差仅比较各自不同成交集合的这一毛函数。未成交机会、费用、库存平仓、标记时点后的损益与市场反应尚缺，不是完整净盈利或因果队列位置定价。

10. **撤单未必改善。** 等概率状态A、B的订单原本全部成交，标记分别-1与+3档。不撤单条件均值和提交贡献均为 `(-1+3)/2=+1档`。错误信号只在B偏空并撤单，成交率变1/2，条件标记降为-1，提交贡献为 `0.5×(-1)=-0.5档`，所以无改善保证。应在独立数据上评估冻结且延迟可行的撤单策略，匹配下单机会，建模撤单成功、队列规则、成交选择、费用、库存和平仓；同时报告全部提交价值与条件成交标记，并处理依赖，不在检验样本上选择规则。

### 资格题两道

11. **中心化微价格定理。** 采用上述有限状态马尔可夫可加模型和首变动绝对奖励可积条件。第一步分析给 `g1=R+Qg1`；因 `rho(Q)<1`，有 `g1=(I-Q)^(-1)R`。将所有不变价等待路径求和，得首次变价后状态律 `B=(I-Q)^(-1)T`。由 `(Q+T)1=1`，可得 `B1=(I-Q)^(-1)(I-Q)1=1`。强马尔可夫性逐次条件化得到 `E_x[M_tau_n]-M_0=sum_(k<n)(B^k g1)(x)`。

    若镜像置换J与B交换且 `Jg1=-g1`，平稳分布唯一性给 `pi'J=pi'`，所以 `pi'g1=pi'Jg1=-pi'g1=0`。无镜像也可能满足此中心化；对称充分而非必要。令 `Pi=1 pi'`，则 `B Pi=Pi B=Pi`，k>=1时 `(B-Pi)^k=B^k-Pi`。`A=B-Pi`的谱由0和B的非平稳特征值组成，模均小于1。有限维Jordan形说明，对任意 `rho(A)<rho<1`，存在有限 `C_rho` 使 `||A^k||<=C_rho rho^k`，多项式Jordan因子被严格谱间隙吸收；一般不能使用恰好 `rho(A)^k`。因为 `Pi g1=0`，作用在g1后有 `B^k g1=A^k g1`，从而绝对收敛。

    Neumann级数为 `g=(I-A)^(-1)g1=(I-B+Pi)^(-1)g1`，且 `pi'g=0`，满足 `g=g1+Bg`。给定第n次变价过去，下一次 `M+g(X)`的条件均值是 `M_tau_n+g1(X_tau_n)+(Bg)(X_tau_n)=M_tau_n+g(X_tau_n)`；奖励可积且有限状态g有界，所以是变价时刻鞅。另一修正f须满足 `(I-B)(f-g)=0`；有限不可约链的最大值原理使调和向量只能常数，所以 `f=g+c1`。平稳均值零固定c=0；有效镜像下反对称性也固定c=0。若 `d=pi'g1`不为零，将奖励写为 `d1+h`，n步期望和含 `nd1`加有界中心化项，因此未中心化极限不存在。该证明不单独推出基本价值、可成交套利或物理时间鞅。

12. **两状态和奇异极限。** 令 `B=[[p,1-p],[1-p,p]]`，0<p<1，平稳分布(1/2,1/2)。H状态下中点以q概率上1整档，否则下1档；L为镜像，因此 `g1=(2q-1)(1,-1)`。反对称向量特征值 `lambda=2p-1`，模小于1，故 `g_H=(2q-1)/(1-lambda)=(2q-1)/[2(1-p)]`，`g_L=-g_H`。p=q=3/4时偏移 **1整档**。固定q>1/2，p趋1时正向发散；p=1链可约，前述中心化逆矩阵论证不再适用。q=1/2时奖励恒零，每个0<p<1的归一化解为零，因此发散论断需要非零奖励。p=0时链周期为2，非零奖励的普通部分和振荡，虽然代数Poisson方程仍有中心化解。负特征值会使项交替，不能当作正持久性。比特币拟合次特征值模约0.668仅在奖励投影到相应正特征模式时支持模式几何倍数，不证明整个偏移统一放大三倍，也不证明市场服从本两状态模型。
