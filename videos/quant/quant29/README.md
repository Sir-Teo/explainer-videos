## Scientific distinctions

Random search draws 10,000 expressions, including repeats. Counting distinct mean ICs rounded to five decimals does not identify distinct signal vectors or independent ideas. A strictly increasing transformation preserves ranks on a shared finite sample; the reciprocal example needs positive, finite inputs. Missingness and ties need separate treatment. A Gaussian maximum scale is an approximation under its own assumptions, not a universal hurdle. A validation/training ratio does not estimate a Gaussian posterior shrinkage parameter.

GP uses absolute training IC minus 0.0005 per node, tournament selection and three seeds. Validation is computed during fitting but is not the selection criterion. Monotone penalised fitness need not imply monotone raw IC. Unique-string caches are per seed, and offspring bred after the last scored generation are unscored. The RL original uses a GRU and REINFORCE over 1,500 episodes; invalid and repeated episodes differ from distinct evaluated strings. Admission requires marginal training-pool IC above 0.0005. Least-squares targets zero-fill missing outcomes, whereas IC scoring filters finite outcomes. The pool evolves during training. This is an AlphaGen-inspired local design, with different assets, horizon and algorithm from the original PPO paper.

The shared-shuffle original scores 500 pre-drawn formulas under 20 daily cross-sectional return permutations. A shared shuffle preserves fixed formula vectors and creates a joint score sample, conditional on that design. Exchangeability of coin labels under a relevant null is an assumption. It does not replay adaptive GP, RL, combination-size selection or all research decisions. Inverting a leading Gaussian maximum scale produces a calibration index; it does not identify independent trials. No extrapolated fraction, corrected strategy hurdle or strategy pass/fail verdict is supplied.

For standardised equicorrelated signals and target, joint covariance feasibility requires

$$\frac{Nc^2}{1+(N-1)\rho}\le1.$$

Thus fixed nonzero target correlation cannot coexist with arbitrarily many mutually uncorrelated signals. A participation ratio counts concentration of sample covariance eigenvalues, not independent trials or causal factors. With a population Pearson projection and residual $e$ orthogonal to the existing library, the additional best squared multiple correlation is

$$\Delta R^2=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(e,r)^2}{\operatorname{Var}(e)\operatorname{Var}(r)},$$

provided both variances are positive. Re-ranking an estimated residual changes this linear-projection problem. The selected empirical libraries also involve selection, covariance estimation, missing-component conventions and regimes that the simple known-covariance model does not isolate.

## Problems 1–6: technical drills, with solutions

**1. Count depth-two grammar trees.** There are six leaves. Five unary plus $7\times4$ time-series choices give 33 unary choices. Four arithmetic and four windowed correlation choices give eight binary choices. Hence

$$a_1=6,\qquad a_2=6+33\cdot6+8\cdot6^2=492.$$

This counts syntactic trees of depth at most two. Algebraic equivalence, repeated draws and equal rankings do not change this syntax count.

**2. Noise mean IC.** Under IID daily noise with standard deviation 0.10 and 500 days, $\sigma_{\bar I}=0.1/\sqrt{500}=0.00447214$. For 10,000 independent Gaussian signed means, the leading one-sided maximum scale is

$$\sigma_{\bar I}\sqrt{2\ln10000}=0.0191941.$$

Selecting either sign uses the leading absolute-maximum reference $\sigma_{\bar I}\sqrt{2\ln20000}=0.0199033$. These are asymptotic reference scales, not exact expectations, rejection quantiles or p-values for the actual formula family. Dependence, heterogeneous distributions and adaptive selection change calibration.

**3. Ideal fundamental-law toy.** Under independent, equal-risk bets with the stipulated unconstrained forecasting/weighting model, $IR\approx IC\sqrt B=0.02\sqrt{100}=0.2$ per day. IID 365-day annualisation gives $0.2\sqrt{365}=3.82099$. One hundred coins are not automatically 100 independent bets; correlations, constraints, selection and costs can invalidate that translation. This is a fixed model calculation.

**4. Equicorrelation.** For standardised signals, the sum has variance $N+N(N-1)\rho$ and covariance $Nc$ with the standardised target. Therefore

$$IC_{EW}=c\sqrt{\frac{N}{1+(N-1)\rho}}.$$

For $N=10000,c=0.01,\rho=0.3$, this is **0.0182553**, and variance breadth is **3.33256**. Joint PSD requires $Nc^2/[1+(N-1)\rho]\le1$. Positive signal covariance alone is insufficient. For $\rho>0$ the feasible large-$N$ multiplier is $1/\sqrt\rho$; its IC cannot exceed one. With $\rho=0$, feasibility requires $Nc^2\le1$.

**5. Parsimony.** Ten extra nodes cost $10\times0.0005=0.005$ in absolute mean-IC fitness. A 0.005 gain exactly compensates, and a larger gain strictly improves fitness if everything else is equal. Selection/ties and the rest of the population still matter. Better penalised fitness need not mean higher raw IC because expression sizes differ.

**6. Agent accounting.** Five hundred hypotheses at four windows yield 2,000 nominal evaluations. The independent Gaussian reference moves from $\sqrt{2\ln500}=3.52551$ to $\sqrt{2\ln2000}=3.89895$, a difference of 0.37344 standard deviations, about a 10.6% increase. Related windows, adaptive prompts, failed runs and human choices need separate accounting and null calibration; 2,000 evaluations do not imply 2,000 independent tests.

## Problems 7–10: worked extensions, with solutions

**7. REINFORCE baseline.** Fix a state and a differentiable normalised policy with fixed support and valid differentiation under the sum/integral. For an action-independent baseline $b$,

$$\mathbb E[b\nabla\log\pi(a)]=b\sum_a\nabla\pi(a)=b\nabla1=0.$$

Treat reward/baseline as fixed when taking the score-function gradient. An action-dependent baseline generally needs a correction. A baseline can increase variance; an appropriate choice, not every constant, reduces it. This identity does not make backtest rewards unbiased or a changing pool stationary.

**8. Equal-notional hedge toy.** Per initial dollar long and dollar short, simple-return P&L is $-0.20-(-0.05)=-0.15$: **15% loss per dollar long**, or 7.5% of initial gross exposure two. This omits basis, beta mismatch, financing, borrow, trading costs and changing positions. It is not a measured fund loss or causal attribution. A leverage multiplier requires a clearly specified capital denominator.

**9. Assumed half-life.** With $IC(t)=0.03\,2^{-t/2}$, strict inequality $IC(t)<0.01$ is equivalent to $t>2\log_2 3=3.169925$ years. Equality holds at the boundary. If observations are restricted to integer years, the first is year four. The chosen exponential law and half-life are assumptions; three selected yearly means do not establish a causal crowding-decay rate.

**10. Plug-in approximation.** For 20 pre-specified independent Gaussian coordinates with daily Sharpe 0.02,

$$S=20(0.02)^2=0.008,\quad SR_*=\sqrt S=0.0894427.$$

At $T=365$, the known-covariance approximation gives

$$f(S,20)=\frac{0.008}{\sqrt{0.008+20/365}}=0.0319249$$

per day. Multiplication by $\sqrt{365}$ gives population optimum **1.70880** and approximation **0.609924** annualised. The latter is not an exact finite-sample expected Sharpe and does not include selected-library or estimated-covariance effects.

## Problems 11–12: qualifying problems, with solutions

**11. Gaussian concentration and finite addition.** For IID $r_t\sim N(\mu,\Sigma)$ with known positive definite $\Sigma$, set $\theta=\Sigma^{-1/2}\mu$ and $z=\Sigma^{-1/2}\hat\mu=\theta+\varepsilon/\sqrt T$, $\varepsilon\sim N(0,I_K)$. The population Sharpe of $\hat w=\Sigma^{-1}\hat\mu$ is exactly

$$SR(\hat w)=\frac{z^\top\theta}{\|z\|}.$$

For deterministic pre-specified $\theta_K$ with $S_K=\|\theta_K\|^2\to S\in(0,\infty)$ and $K/T\to\gamma<\infty$,

$$\mathbb E[z^\top\theta]=S_K,\quad\operatorname{Var}(z^\top\theta)=S_K/T,$$

$$\mathbb E\|z\|^2=S_K+K/T,\quad\operatorname{Var}\|z\|^2=4S_K/T+2K/T^2.$$

Both variances vanish, so Chebyshev and continuous mapping give $SR(\hat w)\to_p S/\sqrt{S+\gamma}$. At finite dimension, replacing ratio components by their means is an approximation, not an exact expectation. The original equal-strength growing-$K$ simulation has growing $S_K$ and is a different illustrative regime. The known-covariance training score using the fitted mean and true variance equals $\|z\|$; its product with population out-of-sample Sharpe is exactly $z^\top\theta$, whose expectation is $S_K$.

For the approximate objective $f(S,K)=S/\sqrt{S+K/T}$, add one fixed independent coordinate with true per-period squared strength $a=s^2$. Positive denominators allow squaring and cross multiplication:

$$f(S+a,K+1)>f(S,K)\iff(TS+K)a^2+(TS^2+2SK)a-S^2>0.$$

For $S>0$, its positive root gives the **exact finite-addition threshold within this approximate objective**:

$$a>\frac{2S}{TS+2K+\sqrt{(TS+2K)^2+4(TS+K)}}.$$

Dropping the positive quadratic term yields $a>S/(TS+2K)$, a stronger first-order sufficient condition, not a necessary exact finite threshold. With $S=0$, any $a>0$ increases the objective. These use true incremental strengths in common observation units; annualised selected Sharpe estimates cannot be inserted as daily population strengths. Estimated covariance adds another problem. For unbiased Gaussian sample covariance, $\mathbb E\hat\Sigma^{-1}=(T-1)\Sigma^{-1}/(T-K-2)$ requires $T>K+2$. Scalar inflation alone leaves Sharpe unchanged; random directional error matters.

**12. Optimal Pearson combination.** Centre and standardise the signals and target; assume finite second moments and positive definite signal correlation matrix $C$. For weights $w$,

$$IC(w)=\frac{w^\top c}{\sqrt{w^\top Cw}}.$$

Cauchy–Schwarz after whitening gives $(w^\top c)^2\le(w^\top Cw)(c^\top C^{-1}c)$. Equality holds for $w\propto C^{-1}c$, with sign chosen for positive correlation. Thus maximal IC is $\sqrt{c^\top C^{-1}c}$. If $c=0$ it is zero. Joint PSD of $\begin{pmatrix}C&c\\c^\top&1\end{pmatrix}$ requires $c^\top C^{-1}c\le1$ by the Schur complement.

For $C=(1-\rho)I+\rho\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top$, positive definiteness requires $-1/(N-1)<\rho<1$. With $c=c_0\mathbf1$, $C^{-1}\mathbf1=\mathbf1/[1+(N-1)\rho]$, so maximal positive IC is $|c_0|\sqrt{N/[1+(N-1)\rho]}$. For $c_0>0$, equal weights attain it, reproducing Problem 4. This is a population Pearson identity; selected sample correlations, nonlinear re-ranking and unknown covariance require additional estimation and validation.

**3．基本定律理想算例。** 在独立、等风险、无约束且满足所指定预测及权重模型的100次押注中，$IR\approx0.02\sqrt{100}=0.2$每天。按365个独立日年化为 $0.2\sqrt{365}=3.82099$。100个币种不自动等于100次独立押注；相关、约束、选择和成本均可能破坏这一换算。此处仅是固定模型算术。

**4．等相关组合。** 标准化信号之和方差为 $N+N(N-1)\rho$，与标准化目标的协方差为 $Nc$，故等权IC为 $c\sqrt{N/[1+(N-1)\rho]}$。代入一万、0.01和0.3得0.0182553，方差广度3.33256。目标也必须满足联合半正定约束 $Nc^2/[1+(N-1)\rho]\le1$。正相关时可行大样本倍数趋向 $1/\sqrt\rho$，IC仍不超过1；零相关时需 $Nc^2\le1$，不能固定非零c而任意增大N。

**5．简约惩罚。** 多十节点增加 $10\times0.0005=0.005$ 的绝对平均IC惩罚。增加0.005刚好抵消，更高才在其他条件相同时严格提高适应度；并列与其余种群仍影响选择。因树长不同，惩罚后适应度更高不意味着原始IC更高。

**6．智能体计数。** 500个假设乘4窗口为2,000次名义评估。独立高斯参考尺度由 $\sqrt{2\ln500}=3.52551$ 升至 $\sqrt{2\ln2000}=3.89895$，增加0.37344个标准差，约10.6%。相关窗口、提示词调整、失败运行与人工决定还需计数；名义评估数不等于独立检验数。

## 中文：习题7—10及完整解答

**7．REINFORCE基线。** 固定状态，策略可微、归一化、支持集固定且可交换求导与求和。若b不依赖所抽动作，$\mathbb E[b\nabla\log\pi(a)]=b\sum_a\nabla\pi(a)=b\nabla1=0$。取分数函数梯度时须把奖励和基线视为固定；动作相关基线通常需要修正。任意基线不保证降方差，合适选择才可能降低。此恒等式不使回测奖励无偏，也不使变化中的池平稳。

**8．等金额对冲算例。** 每初始1美元多头和1美元空头，简单收益损益为 $-0.20-(-0.05)=-0.15$，即每美元多头亏15%，占初始总敞口2的7.5%。基差、贝塔不匹配、借券、融资、成本和动态仓位均被省略；这是固定算例，不能当作真实基金损失或因果归因。使用杠杆倍数还需定义资本分母。

**9．假设的半衰期。** 令 $IC(t)=0.03\,2^{-t/2}$，严格小于0.01等价于 $t>2\log_2 3=3.169925$ 年；边界恰好等于0.01。若只看整数年，首次为第4年。指数形式与两年半衰期是给定假设，三个已选年度均值不能确认拥挤导致的衰减率。

**10．插入式近似。** 二十个预先固定、独立高斯收益坐标每日夏普0.02，则 $S=20(0.02)^2=0.008$，总体最优每日夏普 $\sqrt S=0.0894427$。取365个观测日，近似值为 $0.008/\sqrt{0.008+20/365}=0.0319249$每天。乘 $\sqrt{365}$，总体最优年化1.70880，插入式近似年化0.609924。后者不是有限样本夏普的精确期望，也未包含已选库或协方差估计的影响。

## 中文：习题11—12及完整解答

**11．高斯集中与有限增加。** 对独立同分布高斯收益及已知正定协方差，令 $\theta=\Sigma^{-1/2}\mu$、$z=\Sigma^{-1/2}\hat\mu=\theta+\varepsilon/\sqrt T$，其中 $\varepsilon\sim N(0,I_K)$。权重 $\hat w=\Sigma^{-1}\hat\mu$ 的总体样本外夏普精确为 $z^\top\theta/\|z\|$。

设信号预先固定，确定性强度 $S_K=\|\theta_K\|^2\to S\in(0,\infty)$，$K/T\to\gamma<\infty$。分子期望和方差分别为 $S_K$、$S_K/T$；平方长度期望与方差分别为 $S_K+K/T$、$4S_K/T+2K/T^2$。两方差趋零，由切比雪夫和连续映射得到夏普依概率趋于 $S/\sqrt{S+\gamma}$。有限样本用均值替换比值各项仍只是近似，不能把比值期望等同于均值之比。每坐标强度固定而K增长的原始模拟使总强度增长，属于不同演示情形。使用真实协方差作分母的训练分数等于 $\|z\|$，与总体样本外夏普之积精确等于 $z^\top\theta$，其期望为 $S_K$。

对已属近似的目标 $f(S,K)=S/\sqrt{S+K/T}$，加入真实每周期平方强度为a的一个固定独立坐标。正分母下平方并交叉相乘，增益严格为正当且仅当

$$ (TS+K)a^2+(TS^2+2SK)a-S^2>0.$$

当S为正时，正根给出该近似目标内精确的有限增加条件

$$a>\frac{2S}{TS+2K+\sqrt{(TS+2K)^2+4(TS+K)}}.$$

丢掉正的二次项得到更强的首阶充分条件 $a>S/(TS+2K)$，不是精确有限增加的必要条件。S为零时任意正a都提高目标。必须使用相同观测周期中的真实增量强度，不能把已选年化训练夏普当成每日总体强度。高斯无偏样本协方差的逆矩期望为 $(T-1)\Sigma^{-1}/(T-K-2)$，须 $T>K+2$；纯比例膨胀不改变夏普，随机方向误差才是相关问题。

**12．最佳Pearson组合。** 对中心化、标准化信号和目标及正定相关矩阵C，$IC(w)=w^\top c/\sqrt{w^\top Cw}$。白化后的柯西不等式给出 $(w^\top c)^2\le(w^\top Cw)(c^\top C^{-1}c)$。取 $w\propto C^{-1}c$ 并选择正相关方向达等号，最大IC为 $\sqrt{c^\top C^{-1}c}$；c为零时最大值为零。联合矩阵的Schur补要求 $c^\top C^{-1}c\le1$。

等相关时 $C=(1-\rho)I+\rho\mathbf1\mathbf1^\top$，正定条件为 $-1/(N-1)<\rho<1$。若 $c=c_0\mathbf1$，则 $C^{-1}\mathbf1=\mathbf1/[1+(N-1)\rho]$，最大正IC为 $|c_0|\sqrt{N/[1+(N-1)\rho]}$。正c时等权实现它，重现第4题，并同样满足联合协方差可行性。此为总体Pearson投影恒等式；已选样本相关、非线性重排序和未知协方差另需估计及验证。
