# 经济学，从零搭建：中文术语表

The term guide for the Chinese edition of *Economics, Built*: one Chinese rendering per term, used in the narration
(`econN/zh_lines.py`) and on screen (`economics_text_zh.json`), so that a term reads the same in every part. The
renderings follow the standard terms of Chinese economics textbooks (高鸿业《西方经济学》; the Chinese editions of
Mankiw, Varian and Mas-Colell). Names are given in their usual Chinese form at first mention, with the original in
brackets on screen.
