# Explainer videos

Manim videos with local neural narration. See README.md for layout and build commands.

- Run everything from the repo root; videos are addressed by name (`python tools/build.py quantum`), and
  `explainer/paths.py` (`video_dir`, `video_package`) resolves `videos/<series>/<name>/`. Never hard-code `videos/<name>`.
- A new video goes in `videos/<series>/<name>/` with `manifest.py`, `README.md`, `sNN_*.py` scenes.
- Generated and git-ignored: `media/`, `renders/`, `published/`, `.cache/`. Narration/manim caches are keyed by video
  name and scene module name, so renaming either invalidates them.
- Chinese narration: `videos/<series>/<name>/narration_zh.json`; each bilingual series (`analysis`, `ai`) keeps its on-screen text
  table (`<series>_text_zh.json`) and term guide in `videos/<series>/`.
- *AI, Built* (`videos/ai/`): thirty-one parts (`ai1` … `ai31`); the series plan is `videos/ai/README.md`, the
  chapter-by-chapter syllabus and the lecture standard every part meets are `videos/ai/SYLLABUS.md`; shared
  instruments in `explainer/ai/`.
- *Statistics, Built* (`videos/stats/`): sixty-five parts in five stages from a first course to a PhD
  (`stats1` … `stats65`; Parts 1–26 written); the series plan is `videos/stats/README.md`, the chapter-by-chapter
  syllabus and production standard `videos/stats/SYLLABUS.md`; shared instruments in `explainer/stats/` (the World,
  the Coverage Board, the Museum, the exact probability engine `prob.py`, the `FamilyTree`, the `GaussianSlicer`, the `GaltonBoard`, the `TankFactory`, the `PermutationMachine`, the `IntervalRain`, the `InfluenceProbe`, the `FieldPlot`, the `ChallengerDashboard`, the `BilliardTable`, the `BootstrapResampler`, the `KernelSum`, the `Correlogram`, the `RootPlane`, the `YulePendulum`, the `IslandMap`, the `L1Geometry`, the `Landscape`, the `LikelihoodLandscape`, the `DigitsBoard`, the `DoseResponse`, the `ShrinkagePlot`, the `TauExplorer`, and statkit in `explainer/stats/kit/` (`random`: generators, samplers, the simulation harness; `estimation`: the tank factory game, Newton, observed information; `tests`: the everyday tests, checked against SciPy; `intervals`: binomial intervals with exact coverage, pivots, Fieller, DKW; `ols`: QR least squares with HC SEs, bands, influence, checked against statsmodels, and (Part 23) normal equations vs QR in any precision, Householder, the general linear hypothesis, GLS and Prais–Winsten, Newey–West, cluster-robust SEs and the wild (cluster) bootstrap, Deming/TLS, DFBETAS, B-splines, quantile-regression SEs; `design`: the field-trial simulator, the bakery, ANOVAs, Yates, fractions, Tukey, D-optimality, CUPED; `glm`: logistic/Poisson by IRLS, profile intervals, tables, CMH, ROC/Brier, and (Part 24) families and links, a general IRLS with deviance and quantile residuals, LD50/Fieller, Firth, negative binomial, ZIP by EM, hurdle, multinomial, proportional odds, GEE, log/CRPS scores; `bayes`: conjugate updates, HPD, predictive, grids, Metropolis, Bayes factors, and (Part 26) the hierarchical normal model on a grid with exact draws, normal Bayes factors, the SBB bound, Bayesian linear regression's evidence, SBC, the CRP and stick-breaking; `ranks`: exact signed-rank and Mann–Whitney nulls, Kruskal–Wallis, Spearman, Kendall; `bootstrap`: jackknife, bootstrap, percentile/basic/BCa/bootstrap-t, KDE, K-fold CV; `ts`: ACF/PACF, ARMA roots and simulation, Yule–Walker, Ljung–Box, AR forecasts, Holt–Winters, decomposition, MASE, Newey–West, block bootstrap; `survey`: fpc, Neyman allocation, design effect, Hansen–Hurwitz, Horvitz–Thompson, ratio/GREG, raking, Meng's identity, design-based regression by linearization, the Rao–Wu bootstrap; `learn`: K-fold CV and the one-SE rule, k-NN, Poisson naive Bayes, ridge logistic, CART with cost-complexity pruning, ridge, the lasso path, confusion, PR, AUC; `capstone`: the decision log, restricted cubic splines, reliability, regression calibration; `optim`: Newton with line search, Fisher scoring, quantile MM and LP, a path-recording simplex, the Neyman–Pearson LP, ISTA/FISTA, coordinate descent and the duality gap, ADMM and the fused lasso, SGD with Polyak–Ruppert averaging, PAVA, Bradley–Terry MM; `mle`: mixture EM and Pearson's moments, the trinity, profiles, Neyman–Scott, Poisson sandwich and quasi SEs, the Cauchy MLE, Ising pseudo-likelihood; `shrink`: James–Stein, SURE and exact risk, normal EB, the NPMLE and Tweedie, Fay–Herriot, meta-analysis (DL, I², Hartung–Knapp); `mixed`: LMMs with random slopes by ML/REML with BLUPs, Satterthwaite df, crossed/nested variance components, GLMMs by Laplace/AGQ; the World's `health`, `schools`, `visits` and `hospitals` blocks and the Museum's NHANES extract, Weldon's crabs, Longley, Hubble, Bliss, Efron–Morris, the eight schools and PEAR); `bounds(ax)` gives
  an axis's data range, since `CornerAxes` stores `x_range`/`y_range` shifted to start at 0).
